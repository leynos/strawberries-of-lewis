# Scene Inventory: The Strawberry Bunkers of Lewis

* **Working title:** The Strawberry Bunkers of Lewis
* **Version:** 1.0 (production draft)
* **Date:** 2026-05-05; story period 2038–2050s
* **Logline:** On the Isle of Lewis, a small modular nuclear reactor enables year-round strawberry cultivation in fortified greenhouses, spawning a drone logistics network staffed by young pilots who dreamt of espionage but found purpose in agricultural aviation — until retired signals technicians hijack a cargo drone to prove a point.
* **Scope:** Full (single short film / illustrated narrative)

---

## 1. Creative Pillars

### 1.1 Visual Aesthetic

**Name:** Cold Harboured Light
**Definition:** Atlantic grey exterior world punctured by pockets of engineered warmth — strawberry scarlet, amber grow-light, and screen glow exist as deliberate intrusions against an overwhelmingly cool, desaturated landscape.

* **Palette (5 named colours)**

  * Pewter Sky — flat grey-white of permanent overcast (#B8B8B0)
  * Peat Wet — dark brown-black of saturated moorland (#3A2F28)
  * Strawberry Scarlet — vivid red of ripe fruit, exempt from desaturation (#E63030)
  * Grow-Light Amber — warm gold of artificial summer inside bunkers (#E8A020)
  * Screen Glow Blue — cool blue-green of monitor banks (#4090B0)

* **Lighting rules**

  * Exterior: Always overcast diffuse; no direct sun; no hard shadows on ground
  * Bunker interior: Warm amber grow-light from above; high humidity haze in light shafts
  * Control room: Cool blue-green screen illumination from below; overhead fluorescents off or dim
  * Pre-dawn: Sodium-orange practicals; deep blue-grey surround; rain halos around point sources
  * Drone POV: Flat even illumination; no atmospheric filtering; machine rendering

* **Texture rules**

  * Organic fine-to-medium film grain on all human-camera shots
  * Wet surfaces throughout exteriors: tarmac, stone, peat, glass, metal
  * Concrete and corrugated metal dominate architecture — industrial agricultural
  * No digital-clean aesthetic except drone POV shots

* **Camera grammar**

  * Observation/routine: Locked tripod or slow measured pan; documentary patience
  * Tension/incident: Steadicam or dolly; smooth but purposeful
  * Intimacy/character: Static close-up; hold duration
  * Establishing/geography: High-angle or crane; slow reveal
  * Machine/drone vision: Locked digital; gimbal-stabilised; no human camera characteristics

* **Warmth rules**

  * Warmth appears only inside engineered spaces: bunker interiors, control room desk lamps, cargo pod indicator lights
  * Exterior warmth limited to sodium practicals (pre-dawn) and the SMR's white plume
  * Strawberry Scarlet is the only vivid colour permitted anywhere; it must pop against all surroundings

### 1.2 Storytelling Style

**Name:** Institutional Folklore
**Definition:** Events told in the register of community oral history — observational, wry, unsentimental, allowing objects and spaces to carry emotional weight rather than faces.

* **Narrative rules**

  * Camera observes rather than sympathises; intimacy earned through duration not proximity
  * No hero-shot compositions; characters are part of environments, not isolated from them
  * Objects and spaces given equal screen time to faces
  * Voiceover (if used) is retrospective, wry, community-voiced

* **Rhythm rules**

  * Slow establishing shots bookend sequences; pace increases through action
  * Cut to inserts (props, screens, hands) to create rhythm in dialogue-free passages
  * Drop ambient sound to near-silence for key emotional beats, then restore
  * Weather provides natural rhythm: wind gusts, rain intensification, ferry horns

* **Scale rules**

  * Imply the island's activity through detail: stacked cargo pods, multiple screens, loaded vans
  * Never show "epic" aerial establishing of the full island; keep geography human-scale
  * Collective presence implied through equipment, noise, occupied chairs — not crowd shots

### 1.3 Cinematography Specification

* **Format:** 35 mm (emulated)
* **Stock / sensor:** Kodak Vision3 250D characteristics
* **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (1.78:1)
* **Generation resolution:** 16:9

* **Grain:**
  * Size: Fine-to-medium
  * Distribution: Even with slight concentration in shadows
  * Character: Organic (clumpy, irregular)
  * Exposure response: More visible grain in underexposure; cleaner in well-lit highlights

* **Colour process:** Standard C-41; accurate daylight balance; no cross-processing

* **Colour timing:**
  * Overall bias: Cool-neutral
  * Shadows: Blue-grey with slight teal undertone
  * Midtones: Neutral-cool; desaturated
  * Highlights: Neutral-warm (gentle)
  * Saturation: Pulled 15–20% globally; Strawberry Scarlet exempt (push to full vibrancy)
  * Scene-specific overrides:
    * Bunker interior: Push warmth +20%; amber shadows; humidity haze visible
    * Control room: Cool bias +10%; blue-green shadows from screen wash
    * Pre-dawn exterior: Deep cool; sodium-orange point sources; near-dark surround
    * Drone POV: Remove all timing; flat, clinical, no colour bias

* **Grading:**
  * Blacks: Lifted (10–12 IRE); never crushed
  * Contrast: Low-medium
  * Highlight rolloff: Gentle; preserve cloud detail in overcast skies
  * Shadow detail: Preserved; open shadows throughout

* **Lens language:**
  * Type: Spherical primes
  * Focal range: 28–85 mm; never wider than 24 mm (except drone POV: wide fixed)
  * Aberrations: Mild halation around practical light sources (sodium, grow-lights); gentle warm flare; circular bokeh
  * Vintage/modern: Period-appropriate vintage character; single-coated glass feel; mild softness at edges

* **Depth of field rules:**
  * Establishing/wide: Deep focus throughout
  * Medium/dialogue: Moderate; background soft but recognisable
  * Close-up/insert: Shallow; isolate subject from background
  * Drone POV: Deep focus always (small sensor equivalent)

* **Shutter:** 180° default; no overrides

* **Camera motion grammar:**
  * Observation/routine: Locked tripod or slow pan; documentary weight
  * Tension/pursuit: Steadicam; smooth forward momentum
  * Intimacy/character: Static CU; no movement; hold duration
  * Establishing/geography: Slow crane or high-angle dolly
  * Machine/drone vision: Locked digital; gimbal-stabilised; no organic movement

## 2. Narrative Spine

* **Timeframe:** 2038 (SMR arrival) to approximately 2050 (retrospective narration); core action on a single day circa 2047 (the Great Strawberry Run)

* **Structural approach:** Retrospective chronicle — community oral history compressed to a single peak day, with prologue/epilogue framing the broader transformation. Non-linear internally (setup → escalation → incident → resolution → coda).

* **Theme list (operational)**

  * Infrastructure as character: the reactor, bunkers, and drones are protagonists as much as any human — show how physical systems reshape community identity
  * Glamour rearranged: trained for espionage, delivered to logistics — skill finds purpose where purpose exists, not where it was imagined
  * Island sovereignty over export: Lewis sets terms rather than having things taken away — economic agency through technology
  * Generational transmission: the older islanders calling them "drone kids", the retired technicians teaching the young — knowledge loops between generations
  * Weather as antagonist-collaborator: not background but active participant in every decision and outcome

* **Key turnpoints**

  * SMR activation — Lewis gets baseload; the arithmetic changes
  * Greenhouse proposal — agriculture enters the nuclear-powered future; "Strawberry Bunker One" named
  * UAV network established — gannets solve the transport problem; drone kids become essential
  * The Great Strawberry Run begins — twelve tonnes, Parliament order, pre-dawn loading
  * Gannet Four disappears — mystery/tension; old fantasies resurface
  * Discovery at MOD station — retired technicians revealed; comedy of purpose
  * Resolution — community service as cyber-resilience training; "Operation Dessert Storm"
