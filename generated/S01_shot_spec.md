## Phase 4: Shot Direction

### S01_SH001 — Moorland Establishing Pan

#### 4.1 Actor Direction
N/A — no human subjects.

#### 4.2 Camera Direction
* **Mount:** Tripod (locked tilt, motorised pan head)
* **Starting position:** Ground level, roadside, facing northwest across moor
* **Motion path:** Slow constant-speed pan right, approximately 15° total arc over 8 seconds
* **Ending position:** Same physical position; framing shifted right to reveal horizon right-of-frame
* **Focal length:** 28 mm spherical
* **Depth of field:** Deep — everything sharp from 3m to infinity
* **Shutter:** 180° (standard)

#### 4.3 Lighting Direction
* **Key source:** Overcast sky (flat diffuse ambient from above); no direct sun
* **Fill:** N/A — ambient is the fill; ratio effectively 1:1
* **Practicals:** None
* **Colour temperature:** Pewter Sky — neutral-cool daylight under cloud
* **Grade note:** Global spec; lifted blacks at 10–12 IRE

#### 4.4 Effects Direction
* **Practical effects:** Light rain (misting, not heavy drops)
* **Environmental motion:** Grass bending eastward under constant wind; fence posts vibrating slightly
* **Subject effects:** White steam plume appears at right edge in final 2 seconds — rising vertically, soft-edged, against grey sky

#### 4.5 Audio Direction
* **Bed:** Wind (constant, moderate, left-to-right); light rain on grass
* **Punctuations:** Distant sheep bleat (optional, 3–4s mark)
* **Dialogue:** None
* **Music:** None

## Phase 7: Video Prompt Assembly — Model Routing

### Routing Decisions

| Shot ID | Model | Rationale | Duration | Genre | Mode |
|---------|-------|-----------|----------|-------|------|
| S01_SH001 | `kling3_0` | Landscape establishing; one-vector camera pan; environment-led; no identity requirement | 8s | — | pro |
| S01_SH002 | `seedance_2_0` | Static camera; single dynamic element (plume growth); start+end anchor defines the change; low complexity | 6s | drama | std |
| S01_SH003 | `kling3_0` | Pre-dawn low-light exterior; vehicle motion through frame; environment-led; benefits from scene structure | 6s | — | pro |
| S01_SH004 | `seedance_2_0` | Reference-driven (reactor, engineers); start+end anchor; moderate complexity; identity of the reactor must be consistent | 6s | drama | std |

### Routing Notes

- **S01_SH001:** Model-routing table says Kling 3.0 primary for landscape establishing shots. The slow pan is a single-vector camera move — Kling's camera-language response should handle this cleanly. Duration 8s is within the 8–10s "landscape, one-vector" recommendation.
- **S01_SH002:** Almost static shot; the only motion is plume growth. Seedance 2.0 with strong start+end anchoring should handle this minimal-motion interpolation without introducing unwanted camera movement. The "no cuts, no zoom" prompt negatives will keep POV locked.
- **S01_SH003:** Pre-dawn low-light exterior — routing table says Kling 3.0 primary (low-medium confidence). Vehicle motion left-to-right is a clean single-vector movement. Blue police lights and headlights provide strong visual anchors.
- **S01_SH004:** Medium shot with a specific prop (reactor section) that must be consistent with start/end frames. Seedance 2.0 primary due to reference-heavy requirement. The truck passes through frame — single-direction motion, 6s duration.

### Flattened Generation Prompts

**S01_SH001:**
> Slow pan right across treeless moorland under overcast pewter sky. 28mm lens. Wet peat, fence posts leaning eastward in wind, single-track road in mid-ground. Grass bends in wind. Light rain mists the air. In the final two seconds a white steam plume appears above the horizon at the right edge of frame, rising vertically against grey sky. Deep focus throughout. No trees. No blue sky. No hard shadows. No cuts. No zoom.

**S01_SH002:**
> Static wide shot of treeless moorland horizon. A white steam plume rises vertically from right-centre of frame — the only vertical element in a flat horizontal landscape. Over six seconds the plume grows slightly taller and wider. Pewter overcast sky. Wet grass. Fence posts. Wind moves the grass. Everything else is still except the plume. 50mm lens. Deep focus. No camera movement. No cuts. No zoom. No trees. No blue sky.

**S01_SH003:**
> Pre-dawn darkness. Static wide shot. A convoy of vehicles approaches on a single-track road from left to right. Police escort with blue flashing lights leads. Flatbed trucks carry large cylindrical sections. Vehicles travel on the left side of the road — left-hand traffic. Headlights pierce rain, creating streaks in the beams. Rain halos around blue police lights. Wet road surface reflects headlights and blue lights. Sodium-orange glow from distant strip lighting. Dark moorland on either side. No camera movement. No cuts.

**S01_SH004:**
> Pre-dawn roadside. Static medium shot. A flatbed truck passes camera left to right carrying a large smooth grey cylindrical reactor section. Walking engineers with illuminated tablets visible alongside. Blue police lights flash from escort vehicle behind. Rain streams off the cylinder surface. Wet road surface reflects headlights and blue police lights. Sodium-orange ambient. The truck moves steadily through frame over six seconds. No camera movement. No cuts. Left-hand traffic.
